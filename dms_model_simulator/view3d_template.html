Origin = driver eye point. X forward, Y left, Z up, metres. Hover or tap a point for its coordinates.

Everything drawn is what the simulation uses: the car body, wheel, hand and belt hide what is behind them from a camera; the head is its landmark points.

Camera views here are pinhole: a lens with distortion (dash_right) bends its real image slightly near the edges.
